Bala Game

A little boy from a Kazakh auyl runs and jumps through his childhood, collects candy to stay alive and fights his worst enemies: doctors, broccoli and diapers.
Genre: platform 2D
Platform: iOS 
Orientation: landscape only
2D: hand drawn pixel sprites (Pixilart)
Core loop
Run and jump through the level, then collect candy and coins, after that beat enemies and the boss and unlock the next level (back to run).
Candy is the hero's HP (3 candies), so collecting it keeps the run going.
Controls
Button	Place on screen	What it does
Joystick	Bottom left	move left / right
Jump	Bottom right	jump
Fire	Bottom right, next to Jump	throw an asyk at enemies
Pause	Top right	open the pause menu, time stops

Keyboard for testing on a computer: A/D or arrows to move, W or Space to jump, X to fire, Esc to pause.
